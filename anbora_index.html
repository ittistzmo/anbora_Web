Important Browser PWA Reality & Cloud Protection

No PWA can guarantee 24/7 continuous background GPS tracking when the browser is closed or the phone screen is locked, due to Android Chrome and iOS Safari OS battery & privacy policies.

Instead, ANBORA captures your explicit location whenever you check in or trigger SOS, and relies on Firebase Cloud Functions on Google's servers to monitor your 24-hour safety timer independently of your device.

Privacy & Security

ANBORA is engineered with strict privacy-first principles:

  • Zero Secret Keys in Frontend: All AI API keys, WhatsApp webhook secrets, and service accounts stay exclusively inside Firebase Cloud Functions Secret Manager.
  • User-Scoped Firestore Rules: Only your authenticated UID can read or modify your profile, check-in records, and emergency contacts.
  • Explicit Location Consent: GPS coordinates are accessed only when you explicitly grant browser permission and submit a check-in or SOS.
  • Duplicate Alert Prevention: Idempotency keys in Firestore ensure emergency contacts are never spammed by duplicate alerts.

Install on Android Chrome (No APK Needed)

  1. Open ANBORA over HTTPS in Google Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the Install App button in the ANBORA header, or tap Chrome's ⋮ Menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  3. Confirm installation. ANBORA launches in full-screen standalone mode from your home screen.
